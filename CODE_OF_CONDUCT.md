# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information (including their screen content or API keys) without permission.
- Spamming, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Submitting code or content intended to harm users, such as malware, covert data collection, or ways to bypass the app's safety confirmations.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab (private advisory) or by contacting the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
